Delete your Roadside account

Last updated 3 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Roadside app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email theroadsidebeirut@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Roadside account" and the mobile number on the account. We will confirm by message to that number and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number, email, birthday.
  • Saved addresses.
  • Road Points balance and rewards (these cannot be restored).
  • Roadside Rush scores and your place on the leaderboard.
  • Notification settings and device tokens.

What is kept

Records of past orders and invoices are kept for as long as Lebanese accounting and tax law requires, without your name, phone number or address.
