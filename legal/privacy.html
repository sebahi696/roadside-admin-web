Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

This policy explains what information The Roadside Diner ("Roadside", "we") collects through the Roadside app and website, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Roadside mobile app (Android and iOS) and to ordering through it.

What we collect

InformationWhy we use it
Account details: your name, mobile number, and optionally your email and birthday. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from them.To create your account, verify your number with a one-time code, contact you about your order, and send birthday offers if you add a birthday.
Delivery addresses and location: addresses you save, and your device location when you choose "use my location" (only while the app is in use).To find your nearest branch, check that we deliver to you, calculate the delivery fee and get your order to the right door.
Orders: items, options, notes, branch, totals, payment method, order times and status, ratings and comments you leave.To prepare, deliver and support your orders, show your order history, prevent fraud, and keep the accounting records required by law.
Road Points: points earned, redeemed and expired, and the rewards you use.To run the loyalty program.
Roadside Rush game: scores, coins, distance, run times and the cities you reach.To award Road Points from the game (with daily limits and anti-cheat checks) and show the weekly leaderboard. The leaderboard shows only your first name and a short number, never your phone number.
Device and app information: device type, operating system, app version, language, notification token.To make the app work on your device, send order updates and (if you opt in) offers, and fix problems.
Advertising identifier (only when you choose to watch a rewarded video in the game).To show the optional video ad through Google AdMob. Watching ads is never required to order.

We do not store payment card numbers. Card payments, when available, are handled by the payment provider on its own secure page. Cash payments are recorded only as "cash".

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with services that help us run the app, and only what each needs:

  • Supabase: hosting of our database and accounts (servers in the European Union, Frankfurt).
  • Twilio: sending your verification code by WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, maps and places search, push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and rewarded video ads (AdMob).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and push notifications on iPhone.
  • Our branch staff and drivers: your name, phone, address and order, so they can prepare and deliver it.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Account, addresses, Road Points and game data: while your account is open. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Order and invoice records: kept for as long as Lebanese accounting and tax law requires, with your name and contact details removed once your account is deleted.
  • Verification codes: a few minutes.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details in the app under Profile.
  • Location: you can deny location access and type your address instead.
  • Notifications: turn off offers in the app or in your phone settings. Order updates are part of the service.
  • Delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or as described on our account deletion page.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or anything else about your privacy: theroadsidebeirut@gmail.com.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access inside Roadside is limited by role: for example kitchen staff see orders but not customer history, and only the owner sees financial reports. Every sensitive staff action is logged.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience. Placing an order requires an account, which is intended for people aged 13 and over; younger children should use it with a parent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
